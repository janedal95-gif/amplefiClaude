What we do A practice for the recurring problems no one has time to solve.

Most operational drag isn't one big problem. It's the same small ones, repeated every day.

Teams adapt to problems. They build workarounds, absorb delays, and learn to compensate — until the workaround becomes the process.

  • Delays that recur the same way, every time.
  • Handoffs that break in predictable places.
  • Decisions that wait for people who aren't there.

Amplefi identifies the root cause of each one and removes it directly.

No new systems. No added complexity. Just fewer problems.

How we do it A direct sequence — study, design, install — done on the floor with your team.

Three steps. No overhead, no shelfware.

  1. Operational study

    We work alongside your team to understand how work actually moves — where it flows, where it stalls, and what people do to get around the gaps.

  2. Targeted fixes

    We pinpoint the specific issues causing the most drag and design direct, simple interventions. No frameworks. No generic playbooks.

  3. On-the-floor execution

    We implement changes with your team — not in a deck, not from a distance. We stay until the fix is in place and the problem stops recurring.
